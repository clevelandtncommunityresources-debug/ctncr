About CTNCR

Cleveland, TN Community Resources is a neighbor-led nonprofit serving Cleveland and Bradley County. Finding help shouldn't mean calling ten numbers or knowing the right person. We gather what's available in one place, keep it current, and fill gaps where we can.

We believe dignity, safety and belonging are for everyone, regardless of income, background, faith, gender identity or who you love.

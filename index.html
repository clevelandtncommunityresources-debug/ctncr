About CTNCR

Cleveland, TN Community Resources is just a bunch of your neighbors coming together to meet needs and fight systems that fail us.

We believe in mutual aid: people taking care of each other directly, with dignity and without strings attached. We share resources, raise money for neighbors and for the projects our community needs, and work to change the systems that leave people behind.

Further reading

  • Let This Radicalize You: Organizing and the Revolution of Reciprocal Care by Mariame Kaba and Kelly Hayes
  • Let This Radicalize You: A Workbook
  • Mutual Aid: Building Solidarity During This Crisis (and the Next) by Dean Spade
